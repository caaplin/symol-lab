AquaAlert - the 30-second version

Small farms lose a season's income in one bad night. Big companies see it coming through probes and lab tests; a 1-hectare farm cannot. This screen shows the verdict, why, and the one action that matters - at 05:30, in the farmer's own language.

How it works without sensors

  • Free weather API = the oxygen sensor. Hot, still, rainless nights drive pre-dawn dissolved-oxygen crashes. Open-Meteo gives us that for free, globally, no key.
  • ₹15 ammonia strip + pH + thermometer = the ammonia photometer. We convert total ammonia into toxic NH3 with the FAO/Emerson pH-temperature model.
  • Public pond datasets = the baselines. IoT pond datasets give the normal diurnal shape; the pond's own history gives the drift alarm.
  • Public fish photos = the vet. 2,444 CC0 South-Asia images + 1,043 annotated tilapia images train the photo triage.
